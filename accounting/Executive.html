Sr. Executive - Accounts & Taxation Training

Advanced Compliance, Taxation, Treasury, Financial Analysis & Audit Readiness

Comprehensive Syllabus Covered

📌 Module 1: Advanced Financial Accounting & Closing

  • General Ledger (GL) Governance: Journal entry review, accruals, prepayments, deferred revenue, closing entries.
  • Fixed Asset Management (FAR): Asset capitalization, depreciation rules (Income Tax vs. Companies Act), impairment testing, disposal accounting.
  • Balance Sheet Finalization: Schedule III compliance under Companies Act, Trial balance & ledger scrubbing, Inter-company reconciliations.
  • Accounting Standards: Practical application of key AS / Ind AS (e.g., AS 9 / Ind AS 115 Revenue, AS 19 / Ind AS 116 Leases, AS 2 Inventories).

📌 Module 2: Direct & Indirect Taxation Compliance

  • Goods and Services Tax (GST): Filing GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9/9C reconciliation, ITC reconciliation (GSTR-2B vs. Books), E-invoicing & E-way bills.
  • Direct Tax (TDS/TCS & Income Tax): Deductions, challans (280/281), quarterly returns (Forms 24Q, 26Q, 27Q), Form 26AS & AIS/TIS reconciliation, Advance Tax.
  • Statutory Compliance: PF, ESI, Professional Tax (PT), Gratuity accounting & returns workflow.

📌 Module 3: Working Capital & Treasury Operations

  • Accounts Payable (AP): Vendor aging analysis, payment approvals, 3-way matching (PO, GRN, Invoice), MSME reporting.
  • Accounts Receivable (AR): Credit control, dunning/collection workflows, DSO tracking, bad debt provisioning.
  • Treasury & Banking: Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS), CMA Data preparation, LC, Bank Guarantees (BG), Trade Finance.

📌 Module 4: Financial Analysis & MIS Reporting

  • MIS Dashboards: Monthly cash flow, P&L, budget vs. actual variance analysis.
  • Financial Ratios & Budgeting: Working capital & leverage ratios, rolling forecasts, zero-based budgeting, OPEX/CAPEX tracking.

📌 Module 5: Audit Readiness & Internal Controls

  • Audit Handling: Building audit trails, audit schedules, handling statutory & internal auditor queries.
  • Internal Financial Controls (IFC): SOPs, segregation of duties (SoD), risk matrix tracking.
  • Stock & Inventory Audits: Valuation methods (FIFO, Weighted Avg), physical verification, write-off procedures.

📌 Module 6: ERP & Advanced Productivity Tools

  • Advanced Excel: XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, Pivot Tables, Power Query, Financial modeling basics (Goal Seek, Data Tables).
  • ERP Mastery (Tally Prime / SAP FICO / Zoho): Cost centers, recurring postings, multi-currency & Forex loss/gain.

📌 Module 7: Soft Skills & Team Leadership

  • Communication & Leadership: Responding to tax notices, reviewing junior executives' work, cross-functional coordination.
