Executive - Accounts Training

Level 1: Focus on Operations, Vouching, Daily Accounting, Basic Data Entry & Reconciliation (0–3 Years Exp)

Comprehensive Syllabus Covered

📌 Module 1: Accounting Basics

  • Double entry rules, journal entries, ledger creation, and Trial Balance generation.
  • Voucher creation (Sales, Purchase, Payment, Receipt, Journal Vouchers in Tally/ERP).

📌 Module 2: Accounts Payable & Receivable (AP/AR)

  • Basic Vendor invoice processing and payment matching.
  • Customer ledger entry, follow-ups, and basic AR/AP aging reports.

📌 Module 3: Banking Operations

  • Day-to-day cash and bank transactions.
  • Preparation of basic Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS).

📌 Module 4: Statutory Compliance (Entry Level)

  • Basic understanding of TDS sections (194C, 194J) for applying correct deduction rates during voucher entry.
  • Basic GST invoice requirements and checking vendor GSTIN status.

📌 Module 5: Practical Tools

  • Excel Basics: SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, Basic Filtering, Data Formatting, VLOOKUP.
