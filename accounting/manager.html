Manager - Finance, Accounts & Taxation Training

Strategic Financial Leadership, Advanced Taxation, Budgeting, Advisory & Virtual CFO Operations

Course Syllabus & Topics Covered

1. Strategic Financial Controller Operations & Reporting

  • Executive Financial Review: Consolidation of multi-entity financial statements and group balance sheets.
  • Board & Leadership Presentations: Executive P&L summaries, EBITDA management, and board-deck preparation.
  • Ind AS / IFRS Governance: Strategic compliance, complex revenue recognition policies, and GAAP transition frameworks.
  • Statutory Audits Management: Managing Big 4 / statutory auditors, resolving audit qualifications, and Sign-off workflows.

2. Advanced Tax Planning, Litigations & Assessment

  • GST Litigation & Audits: Handling GST notices (ASMT-10, DRC-01), filing appeals (Form APL-01), and managing GST audits (GSTR-9/9C).
  • Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing: Tax planning strategies, Form 3CD analysis, DTAA, and domestic/international Transfer Pricing compliance.
  • Direct Tax Assessment & Notices: Replying to Income Tax notices u/s 142(1), 148, and handling faceless assessment proceedings.
  • Tax Risk Mitigation: Designing internal tax control policies to ensure 100% statutory compliance across operations.

3. Strategic Treasury, Corporate Finance & Fund Raising

  • Working Capital Management: Advanced cash flow forecasting, optimizing cash conversion cycle (CCC), and liquidity management.
  • Bank Financing & CMA Data: Preparation of detailed CMA data, Project Reports, Term Loan proposals, and Working Capital limits (CC/OD/LC/BG).
  • Capital Budgeting: Evaluating investments using NPV, IRR, Payback Period, and WACC calculations.
  • FX & Hedging Strategies: Managing Foreign Exchange risk, trade finance, import/export compliance (EDPMS/IDPMS).

4. Financial Planning, Analysis (FP&A) & Cost Optimization

  • Budgeting & Zero-Based Budgeting (ZBB): Designing annual operating plans (AOP), departmental budgets, and rolling forecasts.
  • Advanced Variance Analysis: Material, labor, overhead, and margin variance tracking with corrective action plans.
  • Cost Control & Value Engineering: Product pricing models, activity-based costing (ABC), and operational cost reduction strategies.

5. Virtual CFO Services & Business Advisory

  • Virtual CFO Frameworks: Building scalable finance functions, pitch decks, and financial modeling for startups/SMEs.
  • Valuation & M&A Basics: Business valuation metrics (DCF, Multiples), due diligence processes, and deal structuring.
  • Internal Financial Controls (IFC): Designing Risk Control Matrix (RCM), SoD policy implementation, and fraud detection frameworks.

6. Automation, ERP Implementation & Team Management

  • ERP Controls (SAP/Oracle/Tally): Overseeing ERP migrations, workflow authorization setups, and automated reporting.
  • Leadership & Strategy: Leading finance teams, KRA/KPI mapping, cross-departmental alignment, and decision-making frameworks.
