Sr. Executive - Accounts & Taxation Training

Advanced Compliance, Treasury, Taxation, MIS Reporting & Audit Readiness

Course Syllabus & Topics Covered

1. Advanced Financial Accounting & Finalization

  • General Ledger Management: Accrual accounting, prepaid expenses, deferred revenue, and closing entries.
  • Fixed Assets (FAR): Capitalization criteria, depreciation (Companies Act vs. Income Tax Act), disposal, and impairment rules.
  • Balance Sheet Finalization: Trial balance scrubbing, inter-company reconciliations, and Schedule III presentation.
  • Accounting Standards: Practical application of key AS / Ind AS (Revenue Recognition, Leases, Inventory Valuation).

2. Direct & Indirect Taxation

  • GST Compliance: Return filings (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9/9C), e-invoicing, and E-way bill rules.
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC): GSTR-2B vs. books reconciliation, vendor tracking, and ineligible ITC reversal.
  • Direct Tax & TDS: TDS/TCS calculation, monthly challans, quarterly filings (24Q, 26Q), and 26AS/AIS reconciliation.
  • Statutory Compliance: Payroll deductions (PF, ESI, Professional Tax), Gratuity accounting, and Advance Tax estimation.

3. Working Capital & Treasury Operations

  • Accounts Payable (AP): 3-way matching (PO, GRN, Invoice), vendor aging, payment schedules, and MSME reporting.
  • Accounts Receivable (AR): Collection workflows, DSO tracking, credit limit control, and bad debt provisioning.
  • Treasury & Banking: Daily/monthly Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS) and cash flow tracking.
  • Trade Finance: Basics of Letters of Credit (LC), Bank Guarantees (BG), and CMA data preparation for bank limits.

4. Financial Analysis & MIS Reporting

  • MIS Dashboards: Building monthly financial decks (P&L, Cash Flow, Variance Analysis).
  • Budgeting & Forecasting: OPEX/CAPEX tracking, zero-based budgeting basics, and rolling forecasts.
  • Ratio Analysis: Working capital, liquidity, leverage, and profitability metrics for management decision-making.

5. Audit Readiness & Internal Controls

  • Statutory & Tax Audits: Audit schedule preparation, transaction sampling, and handling auditor queries.
  • Internal Controls (IFC): Process SOPs, segregation of duties (SoD), and financial risk matrices.
  • Inventory Control: Physical verification methods, valuation (FIFO/Weighted Avg), and write-off procedures.

6. Advanced Software & Automation

  • Advanced Excel: XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, Pivot Tables, and Power Query for auto-reconciliation.
  • ERP Mastery (SAP / Tally / Zoho): Multi-currency entries, cost center tracking, and automated voucher workflows.

7. Leadership & Communication

  • Team Supervision: Reviewing junior executives' vouchers, reconciliations, and entries.
  • Cross-Functional Alignment: Working with Sales, Purchase, HR, and Legal teams to enforce finance policies.
  • Regulatory Writing: Drafting formal replies for tax notices, auditor letters, and management notes.
